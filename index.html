Privacy Policy for Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate

Effective: October 23, 2026

Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate is developed and published by Parsa. For privacy questions, email goldenvisofficial@gmail.com.

Data collection

Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate has no account system. The developer does not receive users' notes, bookmarks, reading history, or listening history. The app uses Google Mobile Ads to fund its development and Wikimedia Commons to provide optional meditation audio.

Data stored on the device

Reading preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, personal notes, highlights, and the last reading position are stored only on the user's device. They are not sent to the developer or any third party. Android removes this local app data when the app is uninstalled, subject to the device's operating-system and backup settings.

Sharing a verse

When a user taps Share, Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate opens Android's system share sheet with the verse selected by the user. This transfer happens only as a specific, user-initiated action. The destination app selected by the user processes that text under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate uses Google Mobile Ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which may estimate general location), app interactions, diagnostic and app-performance information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This information is encrypted in transit.

Where required, Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request advertising consent before loading ads. When applicable, users can review or change their choices from Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's handling of this information is governed by its own privacy policies.

Internet access and third parties

The Bhagavad Gita text is bundled with the app and works offline. The app connects to Google for advertising and consent services. If a user opens Meditation music, the app connects directly to Wikimedia Commons to stream or download the selected track. Like any internet request, these providers may receive standard technical information such as the user's IP address and user agent under their own privacy policies. Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate does not operate a server and does not receive users' listening activity.

Downloaded tracks are stored in the app's private storage and can be removed from the track menu. Each track displays its creator, source page, and Creative Commons license.

Children

Bhagavad Gita: Read & Meditate is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The effective date above shows when the policy was last revised.
